Historically grounded Han dynasty clothing and hairstyles: topknots with jin caps or guan crowns, cross-collar robes, Han lamellar armor for soldiers. No Ming or Qing elements, no fantasy armor. Calm expressions with strong character. Plain flat pale celadon-gray background (#E3E7DE). No text, no border, no signature, no watermark. Identical framing, scale and soft light from upper left for every portrait.
```
